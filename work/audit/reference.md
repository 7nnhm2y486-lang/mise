# reference.json food-safety audit

No outright dangerous claims found: no canning, fermenting-at-room-temp, garlic-in-oil or unsafe cooling advice. The items below go below US official minimums (USDA/FDA) or are borderline. They are left unchanged; a human should decide whether to add caveats.

1. **temps → "Chicken and turkey" note**: "Breast is safe ... at 68 °C held for one minute." This is roughly consistent with USDA time/temperature pasteurization tables (about 155 °F for under a minute), but only if the whole piece holds that temperature for the full time. Stated as a flat rule, it sits below the 165 °F (74 °C) instant-safe guideline. Suggest a caveat: "held at 68 °C throughout for at least 1 minute; use an accurate thermometer."
2. **temps → "Beef, medium-rare" (52-54 °C, pull at 48-50 °C), "Beef, medium" (57-60 °C), "Lamb" (55-58 °C)**: these are below USDA's 63 °C / 145 °F + 3-min rest for whole cuts. They are standard culinary doneness for intact whole-muscle steaks and roasts, but unsafe for ground, mechanically tenderized, needle-injected or rolled/stuffed meat, which need 71 °C / 160 °F. Suggest adding that caveat.
3. **temps → "Fish" (50-52 °C; salmon "silky" at 45 °C)**: below FDA's 63 °C / 145 °F. Fine for most people with sushi-grade or previously frozen fish (parasite control), but not advisable for pregnant, elderly, very young or immunocompromised people. Suggest a caveat.
4. **ratios → "Pickling liquid" (1:1 vinegar:water)**: fine for refrigerator/quick pickles. It is not a tested recipe for shelf-stable water-bath canning. The text never says canning, but adding "refrigerator pickles only" would prevent misuse.
5. **rescue → "My rice came out gummy"** (spread cooked rice on a tray to dry, then fry): fine, but cooked rice left out at room temperature for long risks *Bacillus cereus*. Optional caveat: cool and refrigerate within 1-2 hours.

Other checks: pork 63 °C + 3-min rest, poultry 74 °C / 165 °F, and "Xylitol is severely toxic to dogs" are correct. °C/°F pairs in `temps` are consistent.

Allergen note (not a safety error): the content assumes nothing about allergies. Items with major allergens that a substitution feature should flag include frangipane/macarons (tree nuts: almond), velveting/pasta/custards/meringue (egg), soup rescue (fish: anchovy; soy; milk: Parmesan), and wheat throughout. The gluten-free and sugar-substitute advice is technique advice only, not a guarantee of allergen safety.
